About me

Breki Sigurðarson

I started my musical journey on the tuba and played in various orchestras until I realised my true passion was to create music. In 2020, I finished my tuba studies and started training in classical musical composition. Afterwards, I continued my pursuit for composing with a master’s in film composition at Malmö’s academy of music in Sweden. There I’ve worked with a number of film makers, game developers as well as film festival executives. My love for stories is closely paralleled with my undying love for music. If you need a composer for your film, TV show, game, advertisement, or other. I will gladly help you! You will find my contact below.
